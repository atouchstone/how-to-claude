How to use
Claude.

AI isn't a chatbot you visit. It's leverage you build into how you work. Three places to put it, a few habits that make it compound, and then you build one.

the frame

A menu,
not a manual.

This isn't a tools tutorial. It's a menu of what to reach for: the kinds of work worth handing to AI, with concrete examples. Take the ideas that fit your job and leave the rest.

Quick show of hands: who used Claude this week?

the one promise

You build one thing

Everyone leaves today having built one thing, not just watched a demo. Set it up in the next half hour and it's running before we break.

the shift

From a chatbot you visit
to leverage you build.

the old way

You go to the AI

Open a tab, ask a question, copy the answer out, repeat tomorrow. Every result is a one-off you have to go and fetch yourself.

the shift

The AI comes to you

You wire it into your work once. It maintains the artifact, runs on a schedule, and reads from real systems. The work happens without you asking.

Example: my day used to open with 45 minutes triaging Slack, making tasks, and remembering follow-ups. Now that runs overnight and I start with a ranked list.

4 · norms

How we make it
compound.

How we work decides how much leverage AI gives us. A few habits make the whole company more AI-powered, not just individuals.

default to public

Work in public channels

Public means AI and people can read, summarize, and learn from it. DMs are dark to the org. It's literally why a daily digest works.

do it twice? capture it

Turn repeats into skills

If you do something more than twice, save the prompt and context so it's reusable. Make it public if your team would use it too.

leave a trail

Write decisions down

Record meetings and put decisions in durable, searchable places so they don't evaporate.

One more habit: AI is wordy. Before you share its output, cut the slop yourself and ask Claude where your doc has holes.

the working session

Now you build one thing.

Pick one from the menu and set it up now. We're floating to help. The best default is a scheduled routine: set it up in the next 20 minutes and it runs today.

recommended default

A morning digest

Claude reads your Slack, inbox, and calendar and posts you a ranked "here's your day." Runs daily, and fires once today.

option

A standing recap or tracker

For a report you currently hand-maintain. Let it re-draft itself from the latest data.

option

A Metabase question

Save a plain-English question for a number you check often, ready to re-run anytime.

option

A coaching or monitoring routine

If you manage people or own a channel, point Claude at the activity and let it watch.

Frozen? Build the morning digest. Then put a 4pm reminder on your calendar to check what it produced.
